Management insight

The favourable revenue variance is misleading on its own. Read against direct cost, it shows a business winning work at a lower effective margin than it planned for.

Payroll and operating expenses both ran ahead of plan while gross profit stood still — so the entire revenue gain was absorbed below the gross profit line.

Recommended action

Break the direct cost variance down by project to separate volume effect from rate effect. Freeze discretionary operating spend until margin recovers to plan.

Management insight

The business is more sensitive to labour cost and collection timing than to a moderate revenue fall. A 10% revenue drop costs 412 of EBITDA; a 30-day collection delay costs over a million in cash and nothing in profit.

Recommended action

Hold a minimum cash buffer sized against the downside case, not the base case. Tighten collection terms before adding fixed cost.

01

Cash Flow Forecast & Working Capital Dashboard

Business problem → forecast → payment and collection decisions
Built for Print Outs · in weekly management use

Cash used to live in a manually updated list — accurate on the day it was written, silent about what was coming. The question management asks is not what the balance is, but what it is going to be.

The forecast builds itself. Receivable and supplier ageing sit in pivots and the forecast pulls straight off them with GETPIVOTDATA, mapping each ageing bucket to the month it falls due — so there is no second set of numbers to maintain. On top sits the analysis layer: collection and payment performance, working capital, cash position trend, and receivable and supplier age distribution.

Decision support

Drives three decisions every week — which supplier is paid first, which customer is chased, and whether a planned purchase can wait.

02

Employee Advance & Gratuity Exposure Model

Business problem → per-employee forecast → exposure management
Built for Print Outs · reviewed monthly with management

A salary advance is an unsecured receivable whose only collateral is that employee's end-of-service gratuity. Management's principle is that nobody should finish their service and find there is nothing left to take home — which means someone has to know, per person, whether the gratuity still covers what is owed.

The model applies UAE mainland gratuity rules — basic salary only, nothing under one year, 21 days per year for the first five and 30 after, pro-rated and capped at two years' basic — against each balance and recovery rate, and returns the month coverage is reached.

Management insight

Gratuity cannot be pooled. One employee's surplus does not cover another's shortfall, so aggregate coverage flatters the position. The model sums each shortfall separately — that is the real unsecured exposure.

03

Compliance & Exposure Tracker

Built and used internally as the HR & Administration Dashboard
Built for Print Outs · also packaged as a reusable template

Renewals used to live in calendar reminders and in memory. That holds until something slips — an expired vehicle registration is a fine and black points, a missed notice window on a lease removes the chance to negotiate, and a credit customer whose trade licence quietly lapsed is a receivable that may never be collected.

One formula-driven workbook now tracks every expiry in the business: employee documents, company licences, leases, insurance policies and customer trade licences on the credit book — with today, tomorrow, 7-day and 30-day tiers plus an overdue list.

Management insight

Expiry dates are money: fines avoided, negotiation windows caught in time, and credit exposure spotted before it becomes bad debt.

Credit exposureCompliance controlRenewal tracking
04

Attendance & Overtime Control

Payroll cost control through a rules engine
Built for Print Outs · feeds the monthly payroll run

Overtime is labour cost, and it is decided by whatever the biometric export happens to say. Rebuilding it by hand each month was slow and it had to be right.

Now the raw export is pasted in and the rest calculates itself — duty hours, overtime, and the monthly summary that goes to payroll. The rules carry the logic: a 45-minute grace period each side for production staff, so early arrival is not paid and late departure is not overtime; every minute on a Sunday or holiday counted as overtime; overnight shifts matched to the prior day and capped at 20 hours, past which it flags manual verification rather than guessing.

Decision support

Turns raw attendance into a labour cost figure management can question — by employee, by department, by month. One paste, and the month is done.

Labour cost controlPayroll inputRules engine

Technical writing & knowledge sharing

UAE Corporate Tax is new, and most accountants filing a return have never filed one before. I file mine, so I wrote down how it works — then built the workbook that goes with it.

01

UAE Corporate Tax Return — EmaraTax Filing Guide

Training material for first-time filers

A walkthrough of all seven EmaraTax steps in portal order, for mainland and free zone companies. It explains what each question means, where the answer comes from, and why it is asked — so the reader can answer correctly even when their situation differs from the examples.

It covers what only shows up once you have actually filed: the double-counting trap in the accounting schedules, the description field that truncates silently, why a greyed-out election is not a bug, and why testing the connected person threshold on a combined total is the error that catches almost everyone.

02

Corporate Tax Master Filing Workbook

The companion working file
Grew out of the filing workbook built for the SteelCorr DMCC return
